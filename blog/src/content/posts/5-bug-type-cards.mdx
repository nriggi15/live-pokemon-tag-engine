# 5 Stunning Bug-Type Pokémon Cards You Might’ve Missed

[← Back to Blog](/blog)

Bug-types are usually overshadowed by the Charizards and Pikachus of the world. But hidden in the archives of the TCG are some serious gems — cards with bold style, rare prints, and nostalgic punch. Here are our favorite 5.

---

### 1. **Heracross** – *Neo Genesis #10*

[<img src="https://images.pokemontcg.io/neo2/10_hires.png" alt="Heracross Neo Genesis" style={{ maxHeight: '300px' }} />](/card/neo2-10)

This Gen II icon gets the full jungle treatment with an intense pose and lush backdrop. Heracross looks like it’s about to charge through the card.

---

### 2. **Scizor ex** – *EX Unseen Forces #108*

[<img src="https://images.pokemontcg.io/ex10/108_hires.png" alt="Scizor ex Unseen Forces" style={{ maxHeight: '300px' }} />](/card/ex10-108)

One of the most elite bug cards ever printed. The metallic reds, angled claws, and classic EX frame make this card scream danger and style.

---

### 3. **Beautifly** – *Evolving Skies #2*

[<img src="https://images.pokemontcg.io/swsh7/2_hires.png" alt="Beautifly Evolving Skies" style={{ maxHeight: '300px' }} />](/card/swsh7-2)

A soft, glowing background gives this card its dreamy feel. The detailed wing art makes it look like it flew right out of a Ghibli movie.

---

### 4. **Parasect** – *Expedition Base Set #87*

[<img src="https://images.pokemontcg.io/ecard1/87_hires.png" alt="Parasect Expedition" style={{ maxHeight: '300px' }} />](/card/ecard1-87)

A perfect blend of retro palette and mysterious energy. This Parasect looks like it wandered out of a haunted forest and onto your deck.

---

### 5. **Genesect** – *Black & White Promo #33*

[<img src="https://images.pokemontcg.io/basep/33_hires.png" alt="Genesect BW Promo" style={{ maxHeight: '300px' }} />](/card/basep-33)

Half bug, half blaster. This mechanical legend has neon eyes, sci-fi energy, and a rare promo print to match.
