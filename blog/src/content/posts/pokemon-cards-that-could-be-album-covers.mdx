# Pokémon Cards That Could Be Album Covers

Some cards have art so dramatic, stylized, or moody that they don’t just look like Pokémon cards — they look like album covers. These five picks capture big sound energy in cardboard form.

---

## 1. Zacian V (Sword & Shield)

[![Zacian V](https://images.pokemontcg.io/swsh1/138.png)](/card/swsh1-138)

**→ View on CardVerse**: [/card/swsh1-138](/card/swsh1-138)

Epic pose. Divine glow. Sword in the foreground. This card has heavy metal vibes and would fit perfectly on the cover of a symphonic rock album.

- **Set:** Sword & Shield  
- **Card #:** 138/202  
- **Genre Vibe:** Epic Power Metal

---

## 2. Charizard V (Champion’s Path – Full Art)

[![Charizard V](https://images.pokemontcg.io/swsh35/79_hires.png)](/card/swsh35-79)

**→ View on CardVerse**: [/card/swsh35-79](/card/swsh35-79)

Centered, intense, and glowing like an inferno. Charizard looks like he’s about to drop the most fire solo of all time.

- **Set:** Champion’s Path  
- **Card #:** 79/73  
- **Genre Vibe:** Classic Hard Rock

---

## 3. Galarian Moltres V (Chilling Reign – Alt Art)

[![Galarian Moltres V](https://images.pokemontcg.io/swsh6/97_hires.png)](/card/swsh6-97)
